Scoring Model

How the 50 points work

🧑‍⚖️

ED Head Review 25 pts

The ED head reviews each RfP and evaluates it out of 25 — the quality of the engagement the customer designed and ran.

🗳️

Team Evaluation 25 pts

Each member of the team evaluates the customer on 5 criteria, 5 marks each. The team average becomes the customer's team score. Only members of the team can access & evaluate their customer.

🏆

Total 50 pts

Team average + ED head evaluation = 50. The Top 3 customers are awarded — same as the Top 3 teams.

ED Head · 25 + Team Average · 25 = 50
5 Criteria × 5 Marks

What you rate your customer on

Rate 1–5 on each criterion (1 = Poor, 5 = Excellent). The evaluation criteria are the same for all customers.
